This is happening at every AYUSH OPD in India, every single day. And the problem is worse for AYUSH than for allopathy, because AYUSH doctors need more information — they need to know your Prakriti, your Nadi, your Agni, your dietary habits — a 60-point assessment that a 5-minute OPD slot simply cannot accommodate.

Our Solution
We built MediKiosk AI — a bilingual, voice-enabled pre-consultation kiosk that the patient uses in the waiting room, before they see the doctor.

It works in 4 modules:

🔵 Module A — Conversational History Engine
The patient arrives, sits at a tablet kiosk, and the system guides them — in Hindi or English, by voice or touch — through the SOCRATES clinical framework (Site, Onset, Character, Radiation, Associated symptoms, Timing, Exacerbating/relieving factors, Severity). Audio guidance reads each question aloud. The patient never needs to know medical terminology.

Simultaneously, a real-time Red-Flag engine scans every word. If someone types "seene mein dard aur saas nahi aa rahi" (chest pain with breathlessness) — the system immediately flags it as a Cardiovascular Emergency, skips OPD queueing, and alerts the dashboard with a priority triage notification. It supports both English medical terms and Hindi equivalents.

🟢 Module B — Document AI & OCR
The patient uploads their old prescriptions and lab reports. The OCR pipeline extracts diagnoses, medications, lab values (flagging abnormals in red), and even checks for herbal-allopathic drug interactions — for example alerting that Yashtimadhu should not be taken alongside Telmisartan as it can affect blood pressure.

🟡 Module C — Physician-Ready Clinical Summary
By the time the patient walks into the room, the doctor's screen already shows: a structured SOCRATES summary, the patient's complete digitized medical timeline, and the pre-filled AYUSH assessment form — specific to whether the patient chose Ayurveda, Homoeopathy, Unani, Siddha, or Yoga & Naturopathy. Our Ayurveda form includes the full Ashtavidha Pariksha. Our Unani form covers Mizaj and Asbab-e-Sitta. Our Homoeopathy form captures Miasmatic impression and Repertory rubrics. Every system, fully represented.

🔴 Module D — Consent & ABDM Compliance
We are fully compliant with the DPDP Act 2023. Consent is explicit, timestamped, bilingual, and granular. Patients can link their ABHA Health ID for future integration with the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission health record system.
